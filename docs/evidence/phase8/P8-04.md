## Implemented scope

The [workload/method inventory](../../phase-8-performance.md) defines seeds, hashes,
full typical/limit and text/history workloads, 24 independent adjacent-cap cases,
template/all-kind/hostile reuse, timing endpoints, privacy rules, reference machine,
warmups/sample/percentile method and same-region bidirectional 100 ms RTT prerequisites.
Prepared harness modes cover real frame/cached offline opening, five users with
matching durable sequence and four rendered peers, and separate limit/10+1 admission.
They are syntax checked only and remain unrun. Final P8-12 must debug any actual
setup/render/socket issues, attach calibration and deployed artifact proof, and
reconcile observed stages; prepared source is not acceptance.

Runtime work is bounded through a cancelable 32-waiter/one-active highlight scheduler
and optional full-source plain rendering beyond the decorative 8,192-character cap.
Canvas memoization with a stable pan callback avoids camera-only shell rerenders.
Existing selection subscriptions, projection caches, authority, geometry commands,
complete candidate validation, exact pending bytes and commit-before-ACK semantics
are retained. Local queue/write and room/worker queue/work durations expose numeric
stages; failed diagnostic sinks cannot break collaboration or saved publication.
